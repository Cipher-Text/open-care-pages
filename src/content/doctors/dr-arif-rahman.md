## About Dr. Arif Rahman

**Dr. Arif Rahman** is a dedicated **Endocrinology & Metabolism specialist** with clinical training at **Bangabandhu Sheikh Mujib Medical University (BSMMU)** and professional experience at the **National Institute of Nuclear Medicine & Allied Sciences (NINMAS)**.

He completed his **MBBS from Khulna Medical College** and is currently an **FCPS Part-2 trainee in Endocrinology & Metabolism**. His clinical interests include **diabetes management, thyroid disorders, hormonal imbalance, and metabolic diseases**, with a strong focus on patient education, lifestyle modification, and long-term disease control.

Dr. Arif believes in **ethical, evidence-based medical practice** and emphasizes clear communication to help patients actively participate in managing their health.
