About Dr. Rahman

Dr. Ayesha Rahman is a highly experienced consultant cardiologist with over 15 years of clinical practice in cardiovascular medicine. She specializes in interventional cardiology, managing complex heart conditions with a patient-centered approach. Dr. Rahman is dedicated to providing compassionate care while utilizing the latest evidence-based treatments to help her patients achieve optimal cardiovascular health.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book an appointment?

You can book an appointment by calling the hospital directly or using their online booking system. We recommend calling ahead to confirm available time slots and to ensure Dr. Rahman will be available on your preferred date.

What should I bring to my first appointment?

Please bring any previous medical records, current medications list, recent test results (ECG, blood work, imaging), your insurance card, and a list of questions or concerns you'd like to discuss.

Does Dr. Rahman accept insurance?

Dr. Rahman works with most major insurance providers in Bangladesh. Please contact the hospital's billing department to verify if your specific insurance plan is accepted.

How long is a typical consultation?

Initial consultations typically last 30-45 minutes to allow for a thorough evaluation. Follow-up appointments are usually 15-20 minutes, though more time can be scheduled if needed for complex cases.
