About Apollo Hospitals Dhaka

Apollo Hospitals Dhaka is a leading multi-specialty tertiary care hospital providing world-class medical services to patients from Bangladesh and across South Asia. With state-of-the-art infrastructure, advanced medical technology, and a team of internationally trained doctors, we are committed to delivering exceptional healthcare outcomes. Our 450-bed facility offers comprehensive medical services across 40+ specialties, supported by modern diagnostic and therapeutic equipment.
